What we found

Reduced visibility in AI shopping answers is limiting qualified discovery traffic. The current campaign reaches a broad audience, but weak topic coverage and generic landing experiences reduce relevance.

Recommended sequence

  1. Establish the AI visibility baseline and competitor gaps.
  2. Create a high-intent recovery audience.
  3. Personalize the landing journey and test the intervention.

Leadership-ready business case

Opportunity: recover qualified traffic while improving conversion from high-intent shoppers.

Needed: shared visibility, journey and audience data; one journey owner; approval for a controlled test.

Measure: AI share of voice, qualified traffic, conversion, revenue per visit and time to launch.
